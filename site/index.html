Services

What we clean

Regular contracts or one-off jobs. Tell us the site and we will scope the work with you on a walk-through.

Commercial & office cleaning

Floors vacuumed and mopped, desks and touch points wiped, kitchens deep cleaned, restrooms sanitised and restocked, bins emptied. Nightly, weekly, fortnightly or monthly.

Floor care

Machine scrubbing, buffing, strip and seal for large floor areas. Commercial floor equipment for warehouses, retail floors and foyers.

Carpet & window cleaning

Carpet extraction for offices and common areas. Interior glass and reachable windows as part of a regular clean, or a dedicated window service.

Builders clean

Post-construction and renovation cleans. Dust, debris and residue removed so the space is ready for handover or fit-out.

Bond & end-of-lease clean

A full top-to-bottom clean when a tenancy ends, worked to the agent's inspection checklist.

Special cleaning

Pressure washing, lawn mowing and pest control arranged alongside your cleaning, so one contact covers the whole site.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

Can you clean outside our trading hours?

Yes. Most of our commercial work runs after hours or overnight. We agree the window with you and the crew is keyed or escorted according to your site rules.

Which areas do you service?

Brisbane and surrounding suburbs. If you are further out, send the address and we will confirm whether we can cover it.

Do you bring your own equipment and chemicals?

Yes. We bring commercial-grade equipment and environmentally safe products. If your site requires specific chemicals, for example in medical or food settings, we match the requirement.

How do I know the clean was actually done?

Every visit has a clock-in and clock-out on site and a completed checklist, signed off by the supervisor. Ask for the shift log at any time.

Is there a minimum contract?

Regular contracts are quoted monthly. One-off jobs such as builders cleans, bond cleans and pressure washing are quoted per job.

Do you speak Korean?

Yes. You can deal with us in Korean or English, in person, by phone and in writing.
